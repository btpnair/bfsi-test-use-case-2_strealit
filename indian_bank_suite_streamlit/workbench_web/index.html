PRIVACY & INTEGRATIONS

A controlled local demonstration

All evidence and identities are fictional. Input screening rejects common identifiers and secrets but cannot detect every kind of personal information. Use synthetic content only.

Sessions use HttpOnly/SameSite cookies, CSRF checks, 10-minute API inactivity and 30-minute maximum duration. Every mutation requires a short-lived, single-use review. Workflow permissions and maker/checker separation are checked again when committing.

Case payload versions, screened rationale, evidence references and pseudonymous actor history are saved locally. Raw passwords and session tokens are not logged. The SQLite database and exports are not encrypted by this application. Case history is retained until local operator reset; common audit entries are pruned after 90 days at startup.

Real deployment requires Bank SSO/MFA, case entitlements, encryption/key management, consent/DLP, immutable audit, approved retention, document/model services, and Bank-approved CBS/CRM/registry/AML/channel connectors. The current connectors are unavailable; no credentials or external actions are simulated as real.
