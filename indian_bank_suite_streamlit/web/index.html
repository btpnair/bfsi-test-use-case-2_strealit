    YOUR FINANCE ASSISTANT

    A little more clarity

    RULES BASED

    Ask about your spending, savings or goals. Answers use only your demo records.

    Do not share identifiers or secrets. Goal changes always need your review and confirmation.

    GOOD TO KNOW

    Helpful. Within limits.

    • Ask a category, then “and last month?” to compare with the prior selected period.
    • Open transaction references to check the evidence.
    • Goals and your last topic persist; full chat text is not saved.
    • No payments, product purchases, guaranteed returns or access to other customers.

    DATA MINIMISATION

    Your information stays local

    • Purpose

      Explore spending insights and planning goals using fictional records only.

    • Not collected

      No PAN, Aadhaar, OTP, PIN, card details, email or mobile number is required. Common patterns are screened and rejected, with no input echoed.

    • Browser access

      HttpOnly session cookie, CSRF checks, 10-minute inactivity timeout and a 30-minute maximum session. Refreshing requires sign-in again.

    • Audit trail

      Goal changes log a reference and version, with a pseudonymous customer subject. Goal names, amounts and raw questions are omitted from audit events.

    YOUR CONTROLS

    Know what is saved

    • Saved locally

      Synthetic transactions, planning goals, the last conversation topic and privacy acknowledgement. Full chat messages are not retained.

    • Retention

      Audit records older than 90 days are pruned at startup. Goals, archives and sample records remain until the local operator resets the database.

    • Screen privacy

      Hide amounts masks displayed financial values. This is a screen convenience, not encryption or protection from someone with device access.

    • Deployment boundary

      The local SQLite database is not encrypted. Pattern screening cannot detect every kind of personal data. Real bank data and public/customer deployment require approved identity, encryption, consent, retention and integration services.
